TTAuOI v2.0.5 — base APK required

For a REAL modified TikTok build, provide the TikTok Android APK that you want to use as the base.

The standalone TTAuOI APK is only the settings/core layer. It cannot become a modified TikTok client without a target APK to patch.

After receiving the APK: inspect package/resources; locate Profile/Privacy UI; insert the Mod entry; integrate TTAuOI settings; add region/filter/theme hooks; rebuild and sign the resulting APK; test installation and launch.
